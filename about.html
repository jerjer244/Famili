About Famili

Every family has a story worth keeping.

Famili is a free, private tool for mapping the people you come from and telling their stories in a book you can print.

Our mission

Make family history simple and beautiful

Heirloom projects often stall because the tools are complicated or tied to subscriptions. Famili keeps the essentials in one place: a clear tree, rich profiles, and an album-style book, without accounts or servers.

FAQ

Good to know

Where is my data stored?

As a guest, in your own browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). If you sign in, projects you save to the cloud are stored in your account and visible only to you and the people or groups you share them with.

How do I back up or move to another device?

Sign in and save to the cloud, or open Create and use Export JSON to download a .famili.json file, then Import JSON on the other device.

What if I clear my browser data?

The local copy is erased, so export a backup regularly to stay safe.

How do I make a PDF of my book?

In the Book section press Print / Save as PDF and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.
